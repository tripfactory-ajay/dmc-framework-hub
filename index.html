What is the DMC Programme?
🏠
Exclusive Ground Partner
One DMC per territory. TripFactory appoints you as its sole Preferred Exclusive Destination Management Partner in your destination. No competitor ground operator is appointed by TripFactory in the same market while you hold Preferred Exclusive Status.
💰
Three Revenue Streams
You earn simultaneously across all three commercial flows: (1) Net Ground Rate margin on inbound bookings; (2) Guaranteed Monthly Retainer for contracting and product intelligence; (3) Outbound Commission (60–70% of Gross Profit) on TripFactory products you sell in your territory.
🏆
Guaranteed Monthly Income
TripFactory pays you a fixed Monthly Retainer regardless of booking volume in any given month. This compensates your operational readiness, contracting function, property inspections, and quarterly product intelligence reporting — your core obligations to TripFactory.
📈
Volume-Linked Bonus
In addition to the Retainer, TripFactory pays a Performance Bonus of 2% to 5% of your total annual inbound Net Ground Rate invoices where you meet or exceed inbound volume targets. Bonus is paid by 28 February following the performance year.
🎒
Co-Branded Marketing
TripFactory features you as its exclusive ground partner on its India website, social media channels, and all destination marketing. You get a dedicated destination landing page live within 60 days of signing — driving brand awareness for your DMC in India’s outbound market.
👥
Named TripFactory Support
A dedicated TripFactory Coordinator handles all inbound booking management (Monday to Saturday, 9am–8pm IST). A dedicated TripFactory BDM drives your outbound sales growth and manages your quarterly commercial reviews.

Preferred Exclusive Status — How It Works
The Core Principle

TripFactory appoints one DMC per Destination Territory. This exclusivity is real and commercially meaningful — TripFactory will not appoint another DMC in the same Destination Territory while the DMC holds Preferred Exclusive Status. However, exclusivity is conditional on performance. This protects TripFactory’s ability to serve its customers while giving the DMC a clear performance incentive to protect its exclusive position.

✓
Preferred Exclusive
Active status. TripFactory directs all Destination Territory inbound bookings to the DMC. No competitor ground operator appointed. Full commercial benefits active including Retainer, Bonus eligibility, and co-branded marketing.
⚠
Improvement Period
Volume fell below 70% of quarterly target for 2 consecutive quarters. 60-day Improvement Period activated. TripFactory provides additional support. No second partner appointed yet — DMC has the opportunity to remedy the shortfall.
↓
Preferred Non-Exclusive
Improvement Period expired without remedy. TripFactory appoints a second Destination Partner with 30 days’ written notice. DMC retains all other rights and obligations but exclusivity is suspended. DMC can apply to restore exclusive status.
Exclusivity Trigger — The 70% Rule
📈 When Exclusivity Is Triggered
The DMC’s actual inbound Ground Services volume (measured by Net Ground Rate invoices confirmed by TripFactory) falls below 70% of the Quarterly Inbound Booking Volume Target in any two consecutive Review Quarters. TripFactory provides a quarterly performance report by the 10th day following the end of each Review Quarter.
🔄 Restoring Exclusive Status
The DMC may apply to restore Preferred Exclusive Status at any time by demonstrating: (a) the volume shortfall that triggered loss of exclusivity has been resolved; (b) the DMC has met or exceeded the Quarterly Inbound Booking Volume Target for two consecutive Review Quarters; (c) the DMC is not in breach of any other obligation under this Agreement. TripFactory will respond to such an application within 30 days.
No Exclusivity Fee
Important — TripFactory Does Not Charge for Exclusivity

TripFactory does not charge the DMC an exclusivity fee. The Monthly Retainer paid by TripFactory to the DMC represents TripFactory’s investment in maintaining an exclusive, operationally ready Destination Partner in the Territory. The DMC’s obligations to maintain that operational readiness — including the Contracting Function, Ground Services standards, and Product Intelligence reporting — are the DMC’s contribution to maintaining exclusivity.

⭐

✅

What TripFactory Looks for in a DMC Partner
✓ Ideal DMC Profile
  • Established destination management company with minimum 3 years’ operating history in the territory
  • Existing supplier relationships with hotels, transfer operators, guides, and activity providers
  • English-speaking management team and operations staff
  • Experience handling Indian outbound tourists (cultural sensitivity, vegetarian options, Hindi-speaking guides)
  • Financial stability to support the inbound booking volume targets in Schedule B
  • Ability to maintain the required insurance cover (USD 2M public liability, USD 1M professional indemnity)
🔸 Required Licences
  • Valid destination management company or inbound tour operator licence in the Destination Territory
  • Commercial vehicle transport operator licences for transfer fleet
  • Licensed tour guides (English-speaking; Hindi-speaking where required)
  • Activity operator licences and safety certifications
  • Outbound sales / travel agency licence where required by local law to sell TripFactory products in destination
  • All insurance certificates current and available to TripFactory on request
